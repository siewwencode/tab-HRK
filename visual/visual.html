index.html control scenarios

This behavior map explains what each button does in the current control-panel interface.

Scenario 01

Mode selector: OFF

The selected panel is locked. Its UP, HOLD/STOP, DOWN, and emergency buttons cannot be pressed.

Scenario 02

Mode selector: MASTER or M&S

The panel becomes available. Its emergency button and HRK movement buttons can be selected.

Scenario 03

UP button

The selected HRK moves upward. Pressing another movement button replaces the current command; the operation buzzer and blue beacon indicate movement.

Scenario 04

DOWN button

The selected HRK moves downward. It continues until HOLD/STOP is pressed or the lower travel limit is reached.

Scenario 05

HOLD / STOP button

The selected HRK stops and its movement buzzer stops. The active movement command is cleared and the beacon turns off.

Scenario 06

Local emergency: press

That panel's assigned HRKs move upward together. Its UP, HOLD/STOP, and DOWN buttons become locked.

Scenario 07

Local emergency: release

The assigned HRKs move downward together. At the lower limit, the buzzer and beacon stop and the panel controls unlock.

Scenario 08

Panel A emergency: press

The activation alarm sounds, the status changes to EMERGENCY CREW ACTIVATED, and HRK1-4 stop. Movement buttons on all panels are locked, and panels B/C emergency buttons cannot be pressed.

Scenario 09

Panel A emergency: limit or release

While pressed, HRK1-4 remain stopped and the alarm continues. On release, HRK1-4 move downward until the lower limit.

Scenario 10

RESET button

All movement stops, emergency states clear, hoists return to the home position, and panels return to their OFF lockout state.

Build notes

  • Use 12 V DC geared motors suitable for a small mock-up.
  • Use one driver channel per motor direction pair; choose a driver rated above the motor stall current.
  • Connect every Arduino ground, driver ground, and sensor ground together.
  • Use normally closed limit switches so a broken wire is detected as a fault.
  • Put the emergency buttons in series with the safety-relay coil. The Arduino input only monitors that chain.
  • Use flyback protection where required and place a fuse close to the motor supply.
